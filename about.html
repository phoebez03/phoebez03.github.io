ABOUT

Hi, I'm Baoyue (Phoebe).

Product designer and researcher — currently at CMU's Human-Computer Interaction Institute.

I care about the why behind every design decision. My background in psychology, education, and HCI means I don't just design interfaces — I think about the human behavior underneath them.

MY DESIGN JOURNEY

From paint to product

I started as a painter. Long before I knew what UX was, I was drawing constantly — trying to capture how things felt, not just how they looked. That instinct never left me.

It led me to study Psychology at the University of Melbourne, where I spent years understanding how people think, feel, and decide. Teaching English to high schoolers on the side taught me something design school never could: that clarity is an act of empathy. If your audience doesn't get it, the problem is yours to fix.

Coming to CMU in the middle of an AI explosion made everything click. I'm not just here to make interfaces — I'm here to figure out how intelligent systems and human behavior can meet somewhere that actually makes sense.

01 / 03

I'm most drawn to products where design genuinely changes what someone is capable of: learning a language, understanding a concept, building a habit. I believe the best learning experiences don't feel like learning at all — they feel like play, or progress, or suddenly seeing something you couldn't see before. That's what I want to make.
